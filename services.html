What to expect

Who is this for?

Mid-sized and large organisations in Canada, the United States, and the United Kingdom. We work with executive sponsors, technology and transformation leaders, and the operational, people, and risk colleagues involved.

Does digital change management need to involve AI?

No. We can help with the people, workflow, and decision-making changes around a digital initiative even when AI is only one part of it, or is not needed.

Can you work with our existing team or provider?

Yes. We can advise leadership and work with your internal technical colleagues or implementation partner. Responsibilities and decision ownership are agreed at the outset.

Will Authentia take responsibility for implementation?

Our consulting offer centres on strategy, evaluation, planning, and implementation guidance. Your team or delivery partner owns execution and operation. Our earlier hands-on work informs the advice.

Do we need an AI project already defined?

No. An assessment can start with an operational problem and the evidence you have. We help establish whether AI is a useful part of the response.

Where do you work?

We work with businesses in Canada, the United States, and the United Kingdom. Working arrangements are agreed around the people and decisions involved.

Do you publish consulting prices?

Projects and retainers are scoped individually. We agree the intended outputs, responsibilities, and commercial terms before an engagement begins.
