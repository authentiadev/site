Workflow and operating model design

Connect people, systems and AI through practical workflows, clear decision rights, useful handoffs and responsibilities your team can implement.

Authentia helps organisations redesign how work moves between people, systems and AI. We map the current process, clarify decisions and exceptions, define future roles and handoffs, and prepare a practical operating model. Your team receives a shared workflow design, responsibility map and transition priorities for implementation.

Design the work around the outcome

Improving one task can change the demands on the rest of a process. Faster drafting may create a review queue. An automated handoff may move incomplete information into another team. A useful AI capability needs a workflow that makes the whole job manageable.

Authentia helps leaders and operational teams define that workflow. We connect the business outcome with the people, systems, information, and decisions needed to achieve it. The resulting design gives your organisation a shared basis for implementation and the responsibilities that follow.

Map what happens today

We work with the people who perform and own the process to understand how work actually moves. What starts it? Which inputs are required? Where does it wait? Who makes decisions, and what happens when a normal route does not fit?

The review considers handoffs between functions as well as individual tasks. We record known volumes, review effort, exceptions, information gaps, and dependencies, with the evidence available. Differences between the documented process and daily practice become questions to resolve with the relevant owners.

Define a future workflow people can use

A future workflow should explain the job of each step and the conditions under which it can proceed. We help define where AI could retrieve, classify, draft, or recommend, and where a person should approve, exercise judgement, or take responsibility for an action.

That includes the less visible parts of the process: how an output is checked, who handles an unfamiliar case, which information may be used, and how work returns to a person when a tool is unavailable. Scenario walkthroughs help the team examine these decisions before committing to implementation.

Connect processes, roles and information

An operating model makes the responsibilities around the workflow explicit. Who owns the process and its outcomes? Who maintains the knowledge the system uses? Who manages access, resolves exceptions, and decides whether a proposed change is acceptable?

We help clarify these relationships across operational, technology, and supporting functions. The design can include decision rights, review arrangements, service expectations, and the information owners need. Where changes affect roles or organisational structures, your leadership and relevant people teams retain those decisions.

What the engagement can produce

  • A current-workflow map with documented friction, dependencies, and evidence gaps.
  • A future-workflow design covering human decisions, AI assistance, handoffs, and exceptions.
  • A responsibility and decision-rights map for the process and its ongoing support.
  • Operating requirements for information, review, access, knowledge maintenance, and service continuity.
  • Transition priorities and a brief that your implementation team or provider can use.

Make the transition practical

We help identify which changes depend on each other and what should be tested first. Some issues may be resolved through clearer ownership or a simpler process. Others may require a technology decision, better information, or a different allocation of work. The recommendation explains those choices and the assumptions behind them.

The workflow design should also inform change management and enablement. Teams need to understand their responsibilities, practise the new tasks, and know how to raise an issue. Governance requirements belong in the design so the process reflects the decisions the organisation has agreed.

Authentia provides analysis, workshops, and recommendations. Your organisation owns implementation, role and structural decisions, technical delivery, and ongoing operation. Start with one important process or a connected set of handoffs; our Workflow Brief Template can help describe the work and the decision you need to make.
