Ongoing AI and digital change advisory

A continuing advisory relationship for leaders connecting AI priorities, digital change, investment decisions, adoption and practical governance.

Authentia provides continuing advice for leaders managing AI priorities and digital change. We help review opportunities, investment assumptions, adoption evidence, governance questions and delivery decisions across initiatives. An agreed remit and working cadence give your team continuity, while your organisation retains authority over investment, implementation and operation.

A consistent point of view as the work develops

AI decisions and digital changes rarely arrive in a neat sequence. A department proposes a new tool while another team is testing a workflow. A provider changes its product. A pilot reveals that the original problem needs a different approach. Leadership needs a way to connect those decisions to business priorities.

Ongoing advisory gives you continuity. Authentia works with your sponsor and relevant colleagues to review progress, challenge assumptions, and identify the decisions that need attention.

Set a useful remit

We begin by agreeing the purpose of the relationship: the business priorities it supports, the people involved, the decisions in scope, and a practical working cadence. The arrangement should fit the volume and complexity of decisions your organisation actually faces.

A retainer can complement a focused strategy project or support work already underway. We agree capacity, availability, deliverables, and responsibilities in the engagement scope.

What we can help with

  • Reviewing new AI opportunities and digital change priorities against an existing roadmap.
  • Preparing leadership discussions and investment decision briefs.
  • Assessing tool proposals and questions from implementation partners.
  • Reviewing pilot findings, adoption challenges, governance questions, and emerging dependencies.
  • Helping teams establish practical guidance for using AI in defined workflows.
  • Keeping a decision record so the reasoning behind choices remains visible.

Maintain a decision rhythm

A useful review starts with what has changed. What evidence arrived? Which assumption failed? What is blocked? Which decision would allow the next piece of work to move? We use that context to keep conversations focused and prepare clear next steps.

Between reviews, agreed advisory work can include examining a proposal, helping shape a brief, or working through an implementation question with the relevant team. The scope determines the level of involvement.

Connect leadership and delivery

Executives need to understand the value, cost, risk, and organisational implications of a choice. Delivery teams need specific requirements, decisions, and access to people who understand the workflow. We help translate between these perspectives and surface unresolved assumptions.

The aim is to leave your organisation with clearer decisions and stronger internal capability. Recommendations should be understandable and reusable, with a record of the evidence and reasoning behind them.

Adapt the roadmap as evidence improves

A roadmap is a set of choices made with the information available at the time. As projects progress, some opportunities will deserve more investment, others may need preparation, and some may cease to be worthwhile. We help review those changes without treating the original plan as a fixed commitment.

We can also help you identify patterns across projects: repeated data problems, unclear ownership, duplicated tools, or training needs that deserve a shared response.

Review value and adoption together

A continuing relationship provides a place to revisit the investment case as evidence arrives. We help sponsors compare expected benefits with operating effort, review capacity, adoption, and the outcomes observed in the workflow. Those reviews can identify a useful adjustment or a reason to change priorities.

We also help connect issues across initiatives. Repeated questions about ownership, manager support, or AI review may deserve a shared response through change management, workflow design, or practical governance.

Agree where advice ends and delivery begins

Your organisation retains responsibility for implementation, deployment, procurement, and operation. Authentia supports those responsibilities through the agreed advisory remit. Where specialist technical, legal, or sector expertise is needed, we help make the question clear for the appropriate owner.

If you are deciding between a focused project and a retainer, start with the next few decisions you expect to make. We can recommend a scope around those needs and explain what continuing support would add.

AI value and investment reviews can form part of the agreed advisory cadence, bringing evidence from individual initiatives into a portfolio-level decision about what to expand, revise, or stop.
