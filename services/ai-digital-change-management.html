AI and digital change management

Prepare people and workflows for new technology with clear ownership, stakeholder alignment, practical enablement and adoption measures.

AI and digital change management prepares people and workflows for new technology. Authentia helps sponsors assess readiness and role impacts, align stakeholders, plan practical communication and learning, and define adoption measures. Your organisation owns delivery; our advisory work gives leaders and programme teams a clear plan and decision support.

Make the change workable for the people doing it

A new tool can be available before teams understand when to use it, how their work changes, or who owns the result. A pilot may demonstrate a useful capability while managers still need a clearer plan for introducing it into daily operations.

AI and digital change management brings these questions into the implementation plan. Authentia helps leaders assess the change, prepare the people affected, and give programme teams a practical way forward. The engagement can support an AI initiative or a related change to a customer platform, internal system, or business process.

Understand readiness and the impact on each role

We begin with the current workflow and the people who perform, manage, and support it. What will change in their tasks and decisions? Which skills, information, or access will they need? What other changes are competing for their attention?

A readiness and impact review makes those differences visible across roles and teams. For AI, it also considers where people must check an output, handle an exception, or decide that the tool is unsuitable for a task. These requirements inform the rollout, learning plan, and support arrangements.

For organisations managing several initiatives, we can help sponsors sequence the changes and identify common enablement needs. This makes demands on the same teams visible and gives programme leads a basis for agreeing practical priorities.

Give sponsors and managers a useful plan

We help sponsors agree the purpose of the change, the decisions that need their involvement, and what managers should be able to explain to their teams. Stakeholder planning identifies the people affected, the concerns to investigate, and the owners who can resolve them.

Communication should answer practical questions: what is changing, why it matters, what people are expected to do, and where they can get help. We can develop a communication approach and manager briefing materials with your programme team, with your organisation responsible for approval and delivery.

Plan learning around the work

People need opportunities to practise the tasks they will actually perform. We help shape role-based learning, realistic examples, and guidance for reviewing AI output or dealing with incomplete information. Leadership sessions can help sponsors and managers work through the decisions their teams will face.

Training belongs within a broader adoption plan. That plan should connect learning with access, workable processes, manager support, feedback, and a clear response when the new approach creates a problem. Our article on AI adoption beyond training explores those connections.

What the engagement can produce

  • A change brief defining the purpose, affected groups, dependencies, and decisions ahead.
  • A readiness and role-impact assessment with priorities for preparation.
  • A sponsor and stakeholder plan with clear ownership and engagement needs.
  • A communication and enablement plan, with manager briefings and practical learning priorities.
  • An adoption review framework covering evidence, feedback, measures, and review responsibilities.

Review adoption and keep a clear owner

Adoption measures should reflect the job the change is meant to improve. Alongside appropriate use, we help teams consider output quality, review effort, service outcomes, exceptions, confidence, and unresolved workarounds. The baseline and review points should be agreed before wider rollout.

Your sponsor and programme leads own implementation, internal communication, learning delivery, and ongoing operation. Authentia provides analysis, facilitation, planning, and decision support within an agreed remit. Where the process itself needs attention, our workflow and operating model design service can help define the work people are being asked to adopt.
