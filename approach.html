Our approach

Start with the work.
Build the case for change.

Useful advice connects business priorities, workflows, and the people who will make a change work.

Understand before recommending

We begin with the problem and the people who live with it. A workflow review makes the current process, constraints, and experience of the teams involved clear. Leadership sets the objective; colleagues close to the work help explain exceptions, handoffs, and the decisions they make each day.

We ask for the evidence already available and identify what is missing. That may include volumes, examples, current systems, review effort, or the effect of other changes on the same teams. Unknowns become part of the assessment and the plan for the next decision.

Assess value and practical fit

We compare plausible responses against the business objective. An existing tool, a process adjustment, an adapted product, or a custom solution may each deserve consideration. The recommendation explains why an approach fits, what it will ask of the organisation, and which assumptions still need testing.

We consider information quality, access, ownership, cost, human involvement, and readiness for change. Expected benefits are connected to a baseline and an owner who can review the evidence. A promising opportunity may need preparation before a pilot or rollout makes sense.

Make the handoff useful

A recommendation needs enough detail for the next owner to act. We help define purpose, scope, information needs, exceptions, evaluation criteria, and responsibilities. Your team or provider should be able to see the next step, the decisions requiring approval, and how the work will be evaluated.

Prepare people and responsibilities

We help review the impact on different roles, the questions sponsors and managers need to answer, and the learning and support teams will need. Communication, practical examples, feedback, and clear escalation routes belong alongside the technical plan.

Our workflow and operating model design and change management services can help turn those requirements into a plan your organisation can use.

Build ownership and review into the plan

For AI initiatives, we help make the questions about approved information, human review, decision rights, and escalation clear. Operational and technical owners work with the relevant legal, privacy, security, and governance colleagues to determine their requirements. Our practical AI governance service supports that coordination.

Evaluate the workflow, including the people

We help teams consider representative inputs, incomplete information, unusual situations, review effort, and the handoff to a person. The evaluation reflects the intended use and the business outcome the change is meant to support.

Adoption evidence and operating effort should inform the next decision alongside technical performance. We help review whether to expand, revise, investigate further, or stop, with assumptions and unresolved questions kept visible.

Keep the client in control

Your organisation owns investment, implementation, procurement, and ongoing operation. Authentia provides analysis, facilitation, planning, and recommendations within an agreed scope. We define the decisions and deliverables before work begins, so your team understands the advice and can act on the next step.
