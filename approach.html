Our approach

Start with the work.
Build the case for change.

Good AI advice connects business priorities, the details of a workflow, and the people who will make a change work.

Understand before recommending

We begin with the problem and the people who live with it. A workflow review helps distinguish a genuine opportunity from a tool looking for somewhere to be used. Leadership sets the objective; colleagues close to the work help explain exceptions, handoffs, and constraints.

We ask for the evidence already available and identify what is missing. That may include volumes, examples, current systems, review effort, or the way a decision is made today. Unknowns become part of the assessment.

Assess value and practical fit

We compare plausible responses against the business objective. An existing tool, a process adjustment, an adapted product, or a custom solution may each deserve consideration. The recommendation should explain why a particular approach fits and which assumptions still need testing.

We look at information quality, systems access, ownership, cost, human involvement, and adoption. An opportunity can have potential and still need preparatory work before a pilot makes sense.

Make the handoff useful

A recommendation needs enough detail for the next owner to act. We help define purpose, scope, information needs, exceptions, evaluation criteria, and responsibilities. The implementation brief should help your team or provider ask better questions and plan delivery.

Evaluate the workflow, including the people

A convincing demonstration does not answer every operational question. We help consider how the workflow handles representative inputs, incomplete information, unusual situations, and human review. The evaluation should reflect the intended use.

We also consider training, feedback, knowledge maintenance, and responsibility for ongoing operation. These decisions belong alongside the technical plan.

Keep the client in control

Your organisation owns the investment and delivery decisions. We bring analysis, structure, and practical guidance. Our recommendations should leave your team able to explain its choices and understand the next step.
