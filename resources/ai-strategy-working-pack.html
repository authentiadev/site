A usable opportunity statement

Describe the person, task, available input, proposed output and human decision retained. For example: “Help a coordinator categorize an internal service request using approved category definitions, with the coordinator confirming every assignment.” This makes scope and review responsibilities visible before choosing a solution.

Assess business value, feasibility, data, ownership, acceptable risk and testability using reasons and evidence. Mark each as promising, needs evidence or blocked. A universal numerical total can hide a consequential unresolved issue. Keep the reason and the next evidence needed alongside the assessment.
