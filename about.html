About Authentia

Practical judgement.
A delivery perspective.

We help leaders make useful AI decisions with a clear view of what implementation will ask of their organisation.

Experience behind the advice

Authentia's work since 2015 spans digital products, financial services, and complex business systems. Working alongside client organisations has given us a practical view of how strategy, design, technology, and operations meet.

Today, our focus is applied AI strategy and consulting: helping organisations identify worthwhile opportunities, plan their next step, and guide implementation decisions.

Our experience includes a dental technology engagement that moved from use-case definition and development to a limited live rollout in Canada, with broader US expansion planned. That experience informs the questions we bring to new advisory work.
