About Authentia

Practical judgement.
A delivery perspective.

We help leaders connect AI and digital change with business priorities, working processes, and the people who will use them.

Experience behind the advice

Authentia's work since 2015 spans digital products, financial services, and complex business systems. Working alongside client organisations has given us a practical view of how strategy, design, technology, and operations meet.

Our services bring that delivery perspective to applied AI strategy, digital change management, and practical advisory. We help leaders choose worthwhile opportunities, design workable processes, prepare people for adoption, and make the responsibilities around the work clear.

Our experience includes a dental technology engagement that moved from use-case definition and development to a limited live rollout in Canada, with broader US expansion planned. That experience informs the questions we bring to new advisory work.

People, workflows and clear ownership

AI and digital change affect how decisions are made and how work moves between teams. A useful plan connects the business case with the future workflow, the people affected, the support they need, and the responsibilities that continue after launch.

Our change management service helps sponsors and programme teams prepare for adoption. Workflow and operating model design clarifies tasks, handoffs, and decision rights. Practical AI governance helps connect everyday guidance and review arrangements with the functions that own the decisions.

We provide analysis, facilitation, planning, and recommendations within an agreed remit. Your organisation retains authority over investment, implementation, and operation, supported by its internal teams and delivery partners.

Consulting across Canada, the US, and the UK

We support mid-sized and larger organisations whose leaders need a clearer direction on AI and digital change. That can mean a focused project around one decision or a continuing advisory relationship connecting priorities across initiatives.

Every organisation brings a different combination of systems, information, people, and constraints. We shape the engagement around that context, with responsibilities, deliverables, and commercial terms agreed before work begins.
